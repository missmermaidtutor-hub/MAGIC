Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

MAGIC — 13 Magical Nights of Art is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

1. Information We Collect

At signup we collect your email address, first name, username, birthdate, and timezone (auto-detected). You may optionally provide last name, gender, and phone number later in your profile. We also collect artwork, writing, and photographs you choose to submit to the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your real name and email are used only for account management and essential communications (email verification, password reset). Your pseudonym — not your real name — is displayed to other users. We use aggregate usage data to improve the app.

3. Anonymity Protection

We maintain a strict separation between your username and your pseudonym. We will never display both to other users simultaneously, and we will never sell or share data that links them. This is a core law of the platform.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Artwork images are stored in Firebase Storage. Firebase is SOC 2 Type II certified and encrypts data in transit and at rest.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We use Sentry for error monitoring (crash reports only — no personal data is included). RevenueCat processes premium subscription transactions on your behalf.

6. Artwork Visibility

Artwork you submit for community ranking is visible to other logged-in participants during the ranking period and, if selected, displayed as a winner in the app. Artwork you add to your Tapestry is visible to other participants attributed to your pseudonym. Your Vault is strictly private and visible only to you.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Deleting your account removes your profile, artworks, votes, and all personal information from our systems within 30 days. To request deletion, use the account settings in the app or contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

MAGIC is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us immediately and we will terminate the account and delete all associated data.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

The web version of MAGIC uses browser local storage to save your session and preferences. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes via in-app notice. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: hello@13magicalnights.com or visit 13magicalnights.com.
